# Motion workflow

1. Analyze: Motion Analysis runs the deterministic engine over entered facts
   and the 154-entry bank. Output lands on the three-lane board:
   Draft Now / Needs More Facts / Not Supported Yet.
2. Draft: open a recommendation (or start blank / from a bank template).
   "Insert standard sections" scaffolds introduction, facts, legal standard,
   argument, and relief with visible placeholders.
3. IRAC: build blocks in the IRAC Builder, link verified facts and verified
   authorities, then "Convert to argument text" - unlinked or unverified
   material is flagged in the generated text, never silently accepted.
4. Verify: pre-filing checks flag unresolved placeholders, missing docket or
   client fields, unverified facts, unverified authorities, open conflicts,
   unreferenced exhibits, and civil Rule 9A references.
5. Approve: moving to "Approved for Filing" requires the approving attorney's
   name; the approval is audited. No other path sets that status.
6. Export: HTML, DOCX, PDF, exhibit packet PDF, or a complete filing ZIP.
7. Record: filing date and court ruling in Full Motions.

Statuses: Recommended, Draft Now, Needs More Facts, Not Supported Yet,
Drafting, Attorney Editing, Facts to Verify, Authorities to Verify, Exhibits
to Review, Ready for Final Review, Approved for Filing, Filed, Denied,
Allowed, Withdrawn, Archived.
