R2 IS THE ONLY KNOWN EXTERNAL ACCOUNT-SIDE BLOCKER
==================================================

The production code expects this bucket:
  arc-legal-files

Your Cloudflare account does not have R2 enabled yet.

Before deploying the Workers:
1. Enable R2 in Cloudflare.
2. Create the bucket exactly as: arc-legal-files

CLI after R2 is enabled:
  npx wrangler r2 bucket create arc-legal-files

Do not put live evidence into ARC until /arc_preflight.html reports that R2 is bound.
