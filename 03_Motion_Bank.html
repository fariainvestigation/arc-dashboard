Letters

    
Massachusetts mitigation and dangerousness hearing letter library. Pick a letter type; basic case information auto-fills from the case, and open boxes take the facts only you can supply. Every draft is for defense counsel review only.


    

      

        
        
      

    


    

      

    


    

    
This letter helps a complaining witness or alleged victim write a voluntary letter to the Court and the attorneys, in the writer's own words. It provides neutral language only and never suggests what the writer should say about the facts.


    

      Read before writing: This letter must be written freely and voluntarily. The writer must not be threatened, pressured, promised anything, or forced to write it. The writer should include only information they personally believe is true. This letter does not cancel a subpoena or court order. The Court, the prosecutor, and the attorneys decide how the case proceeds. The writer should review every word before signing. This tool does not provide legal advice.

ARC Defense Motion Bank is a drafting assistant for Massachusetts criminal defense practitioners. It does not provide legal advice, and generated documents are drafts only: all motions, citations, and factual statements must be reviewed and verified by qualified counsel before filing.

  
Privacy: uploaded reports are processed entirely in this browser. Case data is kept only for this tab session and is removed by Clear Case or closing the tab. Saved attorney profiles and optional API keys (if you choose “Remember in this browser”) are stored locally. CourtListener, Firecrawl, and Google are called only when you use Case Law research tools.
