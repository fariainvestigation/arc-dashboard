ARC Worker secrets — set interactively with Wrangler; never store values in this ZIP.

AI Worker (cloudflare-worker/ai):
  wrangler secret put GOOGLE_API_KEY
  wrangler secret put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY

Legal Worker (cloudflare-worker/legal):
  wrangler secret put COURTLISTENER_API_KEY
  wrangler secret put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY

Research Worker (cloudflare-worker/research):
  wrangler secret put FIRECRAWL_API_KEY

Worker variables (NOT secrets) in each wrangler.toml:
  ACCESS_TEAM_DOMAIN
  ACCESS_AUD
  ALLOWED_ORIGINS

All three Workers use the same D1 database ID. The legal Worker also binds the R2 legal-file bucket.
